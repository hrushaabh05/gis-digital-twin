Project Overview — What This Simulator Does

This digital twin reconstructs the full engineering workflow synthesized from seven GIS papers (D'Souza 2018 · Burow VFTO mitigation · Liu/Dawalibi EMF grounding · Nagarsheth LCC study · Glaubitz CIGRE Earthing · Ametani VFT/EMC · Dodds & Spindle 1975). Walk the 11-step design sequence, then enter the Fault Simulator where cognitive biases (Kahneman–Tversky) and failed falsification tests (Popper) inject realistic failures. Clear each fault using the procedure derived from the papers.
